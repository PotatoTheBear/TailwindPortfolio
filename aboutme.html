About me

Hi, my name is Zoë I’m 24 years old and I’m a third year game development student. During my study I mostly learn to code in C# but I also learn a bit off web development (HTML, CSS, Tailwind). Outside of my studies I like to play games, draw, cook/bake, thrift, watch shows, take photos, listing to music and organize small outing with my friends. Besides this I also really like animals and plants. So I have owned a multiple insect species, plants, fish, crustations, a hamster, a dog and cats. I also put this knowledge to use at my parttime job at a pet store. I also live in the beautiful city of Groningen. Further below this page I will go more in depth about my drawing and photography hobbies.

Drawing

One of my hobbies is drawing, I like to do this both classical and digital. Right now I’m mainly focussing on digital pixel art. Partially because this works well with my study, for example with 2-D projects I can use my own art instead off having to use free to use art. But also because I like how the style looks, blocky, sharp, not necessarily smooth and it also how by changing two pixels you can change the whole look off a thing. For the more classical style I like to draw in a messy style. I love the chaos of it, no line is in the right place, colours go over or don’t fill their space, the imperfection is what I like. I do this hobby on and off depending on how much free time I have and what type off school project I’m working on. Below this article I displayed some of my art.

photography

One of my other hobbies is photography. I currently own a digital camera and a polaroid camera. I mainly like to take pictures of smaller things, like flowers, insects and nature in general. I also take pictures sometimes during outings with my friends. What I mainly like about photography as a hobby is capturing a moment I find meaning in. A breathtaking view or a funny little bug, scary spiders, colourful flowers or a lonely night. I started doing this hobby more since past fall 2025.
